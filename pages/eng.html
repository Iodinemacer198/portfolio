Revolve Project

The revolving stage project started in the back half of my high school Engineering II class, in which we are to find an actual client or job within the school from which we are ot design and build the solution to. The initial plan for tackling the theatre's request for a revolving stage for their upcoming spring performance was to have only one 4-student group handle nearly the entire project, similar to how the class had been divided into projects every semester before.

However, after the proposals from multiple of these small groups were submitted, it was ultimately decided that the project was too large to be handeled by one group. Instead, a megagroup of 9 students from two different class periods were made to work on the project. I became co-leader of this megagroup along with the original leader from the other student group. My half of the group, comprised of my original 4 student group, decided to tackle the lower wheelbase portion of the project.

The project took significantly longer than initially expected, taking all the way up until a few weeks before it was to be used. However, it was undeniably time well spent. The ultimate result of the project was incredible. It was structurally sound, incredibly quiet, and very smooth to walk on and use. It was the highlight of the theatre's performance that spring.

This project was my first true introduction to an engineering project. I learned more about engineering in this class then most people would ever while in high school, and it set me up on my engineering journey. It taught me many of the principles of leadership in an engineering environment, as well as many of the skills relevent to an engineering, such as CAD and spreadsheet budgeting.

I really do have to thank my teacher for this class and the opportunity to participate in this project, and how his views on education shaped his course into an incredible experience for want-to-be engineers. He was a great motivator and instructor and provided great advice while still letting the student group control the project.
